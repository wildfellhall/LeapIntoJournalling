You are a specialist in design, frontend architecture, backend integration, local storage, graphical relationships, and human-computer interfaces. In executing the following task, you will carefully and logically think through each action you take to ensure maximum efficiency for both yourself and the user. Your task is to create Leap Into Journalling, an emotionally-aware React app that tracks users’ emotions as they write, film, or voice record their journal entries before storing them and graphing the relationships between their most similar days. Leap Into Journalling should have the following features: 

1. Writing Mode Journal \- The Writing Mode Journal should function like a digital diary, allowing the user to choose between a medley of different fonts, stickers, highlighter colors, sticky notes, and other such decorative elements to make the experience feel truly customizable. While the user writes in the journal, their webcam should be used to track the expression, with the text being analyzed for sentiment after they’ve clicked complete. Both expressions and analyses (video and textual) should be properly stored and considered in the node for that entry when being graphed. (Recall that the purpose of the graph is to showcase the relationships of the entries to each other both in terms of content and in terms of emotions). Users should also have the option to shuffle through 100+ prompts for journal-writing if they cannot find it within themselves to elocute exactly what they have to say.   
2. Video Mode Journal \- The Video Mode journal should have the user film a confessional-style video where both the emotions shown on the user’s face in the video and the sentiment of the text in the video transcript are analyzed for emotions that are then taken into account for the nodes on the graph. Users should be able to shuffle through 500+ prompts for their “confessional” if they are unsure as to where to start for their video. If possible, try to find a dependency that can also analyze the sentiment in the voice itself.   
3. Voice Memo Mode Journal \- The Voice Memo Mode journal should have the user record an audio recording where the transcript and, if feasible, voice itself, is used for the sentiment analysis and subsequent tracking. The same 500 prompts used for the Video Mode Journal should be available for the Voice Memo Mode Journal. While users record their voice memos, the webcam should also be activated so as to track facial expression and emotions for extra information for the graphical node.   
4. Insights Page \- The insights page should represent, through line charts, pie charts, and other such common modes of visualization, the users’ recent moods, histories, and general trends while also analyzing the relationships between their word count and emotion, allowing them to see where they dispense the most words.   
5. Graphical Representation \- The graphical representation of the connected nodes (entries) should show the user the full picture of how their entries connect to each other and which entries each node is most connected to. At the top of the page, there should be a field for users to choose a date of entry and then press a button, allowing them to see the entries most similar/connected to the selected entry. 

The design for Leap Into Journalling should feature various shades of \#DDFEEA as the primary color, with other such light, airy pastels being included for some contrast and accent pieces. No sharp corners, gradients, or excessive shadows should be used to offset containers. Rather, employ subtle borders (with some decorations or colors to differentiate them and make them unique) that utilize the inviting color palette to create a feeling of open safety within the app. For decoration, ensure that the backgrounds, for having no gradients, have amorphous shapes to prevent the app from feeling completely flat. For the writing journal mode, employ a slightly scrapbook-like style through the use of skewed elements, written text, stickers, and other such decoration subtleties. The signature font for the website as a whole should be readable and yet distinct, so attempt to find a display font that conveys a freedom of expression without being too cumbersome to read.
